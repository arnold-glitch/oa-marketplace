FAQ

Things vendors ask before signing up

Can I see the placement before I pay?
Yes — paste your draft or upload your creative and the preview renders live, exactly as readers will see it. You only check out after you approve what you see.
What if an OA Connect prospect declines?
You're only charged when the prospect accepts. Declines or non-responses within 14 days auto-refund the $300 (or Meeting Credit) to your account.
Who are the BPO executives in the panel?
Managers and above at named BPO firms (Concentrix, TaskUs, Genpact, Sutherland, etc.) plus mid-market BPOs across PH, IN, USA, and other regions. All opted in to receive pitches in exchange for a $50 gift voucher per accepted meeting.
What does an enrichment credit unlock?
One credit reveals a full contact record — email, mobile, LinkedIn URL, and OA-Score™ context. Standard: 50/mo. Premium: 200/mo. Enrichment credits don't roll over; Premium meeting credits do.
What's your refund policy?
Meetings auto-refund on decline. Top-list submissions refund if rejected on review. Editorial placements are non-refundable once published. Subscriptions are non-refundable but cancel anytime to stop the next renewal.
