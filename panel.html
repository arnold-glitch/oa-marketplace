Common questions

How much can I earn?
No cap. Active members complete 2–4 meetings per month ($100–$200). Power users complete 8+ in a single month ($400+). The only requirement: meetings must be relevant to your role — and our matchmaker filters that for you upfront.
What actually happens on the call?
A 30–45 min exploratory conversation. The vendor walks through their offering and how it could help your role or department. You ask questions and share your perspective on the category — without revealing internal strategy or numbers. No pressure to commit to anything. Many calls end with "thanks for your time."
Will my employer find out?
We don't disclose your participation to anyone, including your employer. The $50 is a gift voucher (not salary or contractor pay), but if you're unsure about your employer's policy on outside compensation, use a personal email to sign up.
How do I get paid?
$50 gift voucher emailed to your account within 24 hours of the meeting ending. Spend it, save it, gift it. No invoicing, no W-9, no payout threshold.
Can I pause or quit anytime?
Yes. Lower your acceptance volume, pause your account, or delete it entirely. Your data stays yours — we never sell it to third parties.
