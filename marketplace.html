Bundle FAQ

Can I upgrade mid-year?

Yes — upgrades are pro-rated. Lite → Standard or Premium → no penalty. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal.

What if I don't use all the slots?

Unused SEO articles, article mentions, podcast slots, whitepapers, and top-list submissions roll over up to 3 months after the term ends.

Can I customize the mix?

Premium customers can swap one podcast slot for an additional whitepaper, or one whitepaper for an additional top-list submission — up to once per quarter. Reach out to your success manager.

Is there a refund if our strategy changes?

Lite auto-cancels after the 6-month term unless you renew. Standard and Premium are non-refundable but you can pause publication and bank the unused slots for the rollover window.

Is there an enterprise tier above Premium?

Yes — for vendors needing 48+ SEO articles, custom podcast series, or large-volume display campaigns, we offer custom enterprise contracts. Contact enterprise@outsourceaccelerator.com for a quote.
