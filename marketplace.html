FAQ

The short version. Bundle-specific terms live on the Premium Bundle tab.

Do I have to pay before I see the placement?

No. Every product renders a live preview inside its builder, and you can iterate as many times as you like. Nothing enters the editorial queue and your card isn’t charged until you approve.

Which products can I just buy, and which need an inquiry?

Guest Posting, Link Insertion, Newsletter Insertion and Display Ads are instant — build, preview, check out. Top List Inclusion, Podcast Interview and Co-branded Whitepaper depend on editorial fit, scheduling or scope, so you submit a request and we confirm availability and final price before anything is charged. Bundles start with a call.

How quickly does a placement go live?

It depends on the format. Guest Posting is 3 business days and Link Insertion 3–5. Top List Inclusion gets an editorial decision in 5 business days. Newsletter Insertion starts with the next edition and Display Ads go up in about 3 business days. Podcast Interviews are booked around the recording calendar, and whitepapers take 2–3 months end to end.

Which placements are permanent, and which run for a term?

Guest Posting, Link Insertion, Top List Inclusion and podcast show notes stay up permanently — there’s no monthly fee and nothing rotates out. Newsletter Insertion runs for one month, Display Ads for a quarter, and the whitepaper campaign for three months, though the whitepaper itself stays hosted on OA.

Can I choose which article my link goes into?

Yes. With Link Insertion you pick the target article and the anchor keyword, and OA editorial places it naturally in-context. Listicles and “Top N” list-of-services articles aren’t eligible — those are handled through Top List Inclusion instead.

Who writes the content?

You do, for most formats — you supply the guest-post draft, the newsletter blurb and the ad creative, and OA editorial edits and formats them. Co-branded Whitepaper is the exception: OA researches, writes and designs the whole 12–18 page report with you.

Is Top List Inclusion a guaranteed placement?

No — it’s a paid submission to an editorially curated list. Decisions come back in 5 business days, and if your submission is rejected the fee is refunded in full.

Can I upgrade from à la carte to a bundle later?

Yes. Bundle upgrades are pro-rated — Lite to Standard or Premium carries no penalty. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal.

What happens to slots I don’t use?

Unused articles, podcast slots, whitepapers and top-list submissions roll over for up to 3 months after the term ends.

Still have questions? Book a call with us.

If you’re not sure which format fits — or you want a mix priced up as a bundle — a 20-minute call is the fastest way to work it out. We’ll walk through what’s worked for vendors in your category and send a proposal afterwards.
