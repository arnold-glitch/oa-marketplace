Vendor FAQ

What if no one accepts my pitch?

Every prospect who declines or doesn't respond within 14 days auto-refunds the $300. Worst case: you get all your money back and learn your ICP needs refinement. We'll also give you a 30-min consult with our matchmaker for free if your acceptance rate is below 30%.

How accurate is the matchmaker?

Match scores combine the prospect's stated focus areas with their LinkedIn-sourced role, seniority, employer, and recent activity. Strong matches (70%+) historically convert to acceptance ~85% of the time. Medium matches (40-70%) ~55%. Below 40% we don't reach out at all.

What if the meeting is a waste of time?

Rate the meeting after. 1-star meetings are reviewed by our quality team. Pattern issues (e.g. a prospect repeatedly mismatching pitch claims) result in panel removal. For one-off poor fits, we don't refund — but your rating informs the matchmaker so it doesn't happen again.

Can I book the same prospect more than once?

Not within 6 months of a previous booking with that prospect. After that window, you can rebook. Most successful follow-ups happen off-platform after the first discovery call connects you directly.

How is this priced if I want bulk?

$300 is the unit price per accepted meeting. Premium tier ($499/mo) includes 2 meeting credits per month that roll over indefinitely — so heavy users get a discount. Reach out to sales for enterprise bulk pricing (50+ meetings/month).
