→ Once a lead is captured, which engagement track?
If has WhatsApp opt-in
WA-FIRST TRACK

Default for PH / India / LatAm. Opt-in earned via CTWA ad click, form checkbox, or inbound reply. WhatsApp template fires Day 2, voice note on reply, email serves as backup. Highest reply rates (25–40% in PH/IN).

If no WhatsApp opt-in
EMAIL-FIRST TRACK

Default for US / UK / global. Email leads, SMS at days 5/10/17, LinkedIn DM in parallel. Sequence actively tries to earn WA opt-in mid-flight (reply prompt in E2 and E3). Standard 3–8% reply rates.

Rule 1 — No overlap Email and SMS never fire on the same day. WhatsApp templates never within 24h of another WA send.
Rule 2 — Reply halts everything Any inbound on any channel pauses all queued sequences for that contact. CRM-enforced from day one.
Rule 3 — Signup exits Stage 2 Registered panelists immediately drop out of cold sequences and enter the activation track.

Stage 3

Conversion — from signup to first $50

Once a lead clicks through and registers, the goal is one accepted call within their first week. Profile completion is the leading indicator.

1

Email + role + LinkedIn (or CV upload). 30 seconds.

2

3

30–45 min discovery. No pressure, no demos required.

Stage 4

Activation & retention — keep them taking calls

Lifetime value depends on monthly recurring meetings. Four touchpoints keep active panelists engaged without spamming.

👥

WhatsApp Community

"OA Panel Insiders" — announcements, monthly leaderboard, panelist-to-panelist sub-group. Exclusive feel, network effect.

📲

WA Status (2–3×/week)

Earnings screenshots (anonymized), new vendor categories, short YouTube clips, FAQ answers. Stays top-of-mind.

📰

Monthly Email

"Vendors this month" digest + earnings recap. Soft re-engagement for panelists who haven't accepted recently.

🔔

Expiry Nudges

"2 pitches expire in 5 days · $100 potential" — delivered in-app, by WA, and email. Drives accept-rate above 60%.

Measurement

KPIs tracked weekly

Five buckets. Health metrics are non-negotiable — if email deliverability or WA quality drops, slow the volume until it recovers.

Acquisition
  • Cost per lead, by channel
  • CTWA chat-start rate
  • Landing page conversion rate
Engagement
  • Reply rate by channel
  • Sequence completion rate
  • Channel mix per signup
Conversion
  • Signup rate (lead → register)
  • Profile completion rate
  • Time to first pitch matched
Activation
  • Time-to-first-accepted-call
  • % panelists with 1+ call/mo
  • Vendor satisfaction rating
Retention
  • Monthly active panelists
  • Avg meetings per panelist
  • 30 / 60 / 90-day churn
Health
  • Email deliverability (Inbox / Spam)
  • WA quality rating (High / Med / Low)
  • LinkedIn account restrictions
4-week ramp

Rollout schedule before full launch

Most of week 1–3 is infrastructure that earns nothing yet but underwrites every subsequent week of sending. Don't skip warmup.

Week 1

Foundation

  • Buy 3–4 lookalike domains
  • Set up 9–12 inboxes · start warmup
  • Pick WhatsApp BSP, submit 4 templates
  • Create FB / IG / YouTube pages
  • Brief paid-media specialist
Week 2

Creative

  • Record YouTube video (90s + shorts)
  • Build email / SMS / WA sequences in tool
  • Design FB ad creative · 3 variants
  • WhatsApp templates approved by Meta
  • Draft LinkedIn DM scripts
Week 3

Data + QA

  • Source first 500-contact list (Sales Nav + Clay)
  • End-to-end QA all 6 channels
  • Set up dashboards + reply-halt logic
  • Soft-test with 10 internal contacts
  • Train SDRs on inbox monitoring
Week 4

Launch

  • Inboxes warm → send at 30/day each
  • Launch FB direct + Click-to-WA ads
  • Begin LinkedIn outreach (2–3 SDRs)
  • Publish YouTube video + shorts
  • Ramp weekly based on quality metrics
